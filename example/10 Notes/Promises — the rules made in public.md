# Promises — the rules made in public

The rules the author has stated on the record. Every draft must honour these; a draft that would break one should say so instead.

1. **No recipe goes out until it has been baked three times.** Stated in Post 01. A work note marks a recipe *baked ×3* when it qualifies.
2. **Every number says whose it is.** Own kitchen, a named book, or a study with a citation. "Everyone knows" is not a source. Stated in Post 01, repeated in the reply to [[@quickrise]] on Post 02.
3. **Mistakes stay in.** A post is not edited to remove a failure after the fact; a correction is added below it, dated. Stated in Post 02.
