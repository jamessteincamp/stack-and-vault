# Working in this vault

This folder is a small Zettelkasten built from *The Starter Log*, a short-lived blog about keeping a sourdough starter. It exists as a worked example of the method in the toolkit one level up: the subject is deliberately unglamorous so that the shape of the vault, not the content, is what you notice. The blog, its author and its commenters are invented.

## State
2 posts processed. `00 Inbox/` is empty. 20 notes. Feedback on Post 02 captured 2026-09-05; the week of 2026-08-31 has been synthesised.

## Conventions
This vault follows the conventions in `../vault/CLAUDE.md` exactly: one idea per note, the title is the claim, five sections per zettel, the `evidence:` field, source notes named after the source, *In the work* separate from *Outside the work*. The routines (`Inbox — how to empty it`, `Triage — the evidence field`, the research routine) live in `../vault/20 Maps/` and are not duplicated here.

## What this example demonstrates
Every convention in the toolkit has exactly one worked instance here: a zettel with a real boundary, an `argument` note that cannot be sourced, a `partial` source note whose verbatim line is still missing, a `needs-source` placeholder named for its claim, an objection carrying the author's own reply, a pack, a daily note, a map with live tensions, and a weekly synthesis. The synthesis is the one to read if you only read one file: it is where five separate notes turn into a revision of the thesis.

## Hard rule
The author has said in public that no recipe goes out until it has been baked three times. A draft that gives a recipe not yet marked *baked ×3* in a work note breaks that promise; say so rather than drafting it.

## Do not
- Do not merge notes into long documents.
- Do not invent a citation. [[Rye speeds fermentation]] is a placeholder for a source that has not been found; leave it that way until it is.
