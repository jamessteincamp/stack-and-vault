# Hooch

The layer of grey or brownish liquid that forms on top of a starter that has gone unfed for long enough to exhaust its flour. Mostly water and alcohol, by the usual account; smells sharp. The word is the author's, borrowed from the wider sourdough vocabulary, and is used in Post 02 without explanation, which the feedback noticed.

Gloss on first use in any draft: *hooch, the liquid that forms on a hungry starter*.
