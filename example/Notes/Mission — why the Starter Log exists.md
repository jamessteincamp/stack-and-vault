# Mission — why the Starter Log exists

The blog exists to record one person learning to keep a sourdough starter alive, in public, with the mistakes left in. It is not a recipe site. Its argument, stated in the first post, is that most starter advice fails because it gives schedules where it should give signals: "feed every twelve hours" instead of "feed when it has peaked and begun to fall."

What would count as success: a reader who has killed two starters keeps the third, and can say why.

What it refuses to do: publish a recipe that has not been baked three times; state a number without saying whose it is.
