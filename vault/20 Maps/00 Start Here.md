## Where things live
Six folders, by stage of work. The kind of a note is in its frontmatter, not its folder.
- **Inbox** → raw material awaiting processing. [[Inbox — how to empty it]] explains the routine.
- **30 Daily** → one per working day.
- **Notes** → the ideas (title is the claim), vocabulary, people, objections, and the mission notes.
- **Maps** → this page, the indexes ([[Objections — index]], [[Sources — index]]), the series hubs, the ledger ([[Commitments — promises made in public]]), the routines, and the briefing packs ([[How to brief an assistant from this vault]]).
- **Sources** → the works, their full texts, the feedback captures, and the citation notes.
- **Templates** → one per kind of note.

**Using this alongside a teammate stack?** [[Working with the teammate stack]] is the contract between them.
